## Formats that work

1. **Text + one image** — architecture diagram (draw in Excalidraw/draw.io from the ASCII in the project files) or a blurred screenshot.
2. **Carousel (PDF)** — 8–10 slides: title, problem, design, hard part, numbers, lesson. Good for Post 2, 5 and 9.
3. **Short video (30–60 s)** — screen recording of the QMS recording player or ViewReports sync console with test data.
4. **Poll** — "SQLite as a server-side read-model: smart or scary?" → drives comments.

## Engagement tips

- Reply to every comment within the first hour; ask a follow-up question.
- Tag no one you don't know; do tag tools/companies only when relevant (e.g., Exotel, Cloudways) and truthful.
- Reuse content: each post → a carousel slide → a portfolio case study → an interview story.
- Keep the first line under 120 characters; no hashtags in the first line.
- Never post internal hostnames, database names, phone numbers, WABA IDs, API keys or customer data. Blur screenshots.
- Add a comment under your own post with links (portfolio/GitHub) instead of putting them in the body (better reach).
